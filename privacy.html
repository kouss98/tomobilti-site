Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Also in français and العربية

Who we are

Tomobilti is a mileage and car maintenance tracker. Contact: kousssa66@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and profile picture from Google sign-in (or your email address if you sign in with an email code).
  • Cars and maintenance: the cars you add, odometer readings, services and reminders you enter.
  • Trips: distance, start and end time, speed, and start/end places rounded to about 100 metres, plus labels you add. Trips are recorded only while you use the recorder, with your permission to access location while the app is in use.
  • Sharing: members and roles of the cars you share, and invite codes.

What stays on your phone

Raw GPS points never leave your phone. They are deleted after a trip is saved. A simplified route is kept only on the phone that recorded the trip and is never uploaded.

How we use it

To run the app: show your cars, trips and reminders, sync them between your devices, and let people you share a car with see that car's data according to their role. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service provider Supabase (database region: London, United Kingdom). Sign-in uses Google Sign-In. Sign-in emails are sent through an email delivery provider. Error reports, if enabled, do not contain location data.

Your choices

  • Export your data from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings. This removes your profile, devices and memberships. On cars you share, your past trips are anonymised so the car's odometer history stays correct.
  • To ask for deletion by email, write to kousssa66@gmail.com from the address on your account.

Children

Tomobilti is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and, for significant changes, we tell you in the app.

Politique de confidentialité (résumé)

Tomobilti enregistre vos voitures, kilométrages, entretiens et trajets (distance, durées, vitesses, lieux de départ/arrivée arrondis à environ 100 m). Les points GPS bruts ne quittent jamais votre téléphone et sont supprimés après l’enregistrement du trajet. Les données sont stockées chez Supabase (Londres). Nous ne vendons pas vos données et n’affichons pas de publicité. Vous pouvez exporter vos données et supprimer votre compte depuis les Réglages, ou en écrivant à kousssa66@gmail.com.
